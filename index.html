✦ FAQ

Questions
answered

How fast is video rendering? ▼
VisionForge uses a distributed GPU cloud with thousands of dedicated nodes. Standard videos render in 6–12 seconds. Pro users get priority queue access with sub-6s render times, even for 4K resolution outputs.
Can I use videos commercially? ▼
Yes! Pro and Enterprise plans include a full commercial license. You own your generated content completely — sell it, broadcast it, use it in client projects, monetize it on platforms. No royalties, no restrictions.
Does VisionForge support 4K export? ▼
Absolutely. Pro and Enterprise plans support 4K Ultra HD (3840×2160) export in H.264, H.265, and ProRes formats. Enterprise users can also access RAW exports with full color grading data for post-production workflows.
What AI models power VisionForge? ▼
VisionForge is built on our proprietary VF-Forge v3.5 video generation model, trained on over 500 million video frames. It's combined with our CineMotion™ physics engine and VoiceSync™ audio model to deliver cohesive, cinematic results.
Can I upload my own images or videos? ▼
Yes! Our Image-to-Video animator lets you upload any image (PNG, JPG, WebP) and bring it to life with AI motion. Pro users can also upload reference videos for style transfer and scene continuation features.
